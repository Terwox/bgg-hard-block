One-time setup

Make BGG blocks actually disappear.

BGG Hard Block needs your permission before it processes anything on BoardGameGeek.

What the extension handles

  • Your BGG Hidden Users list and the public usernames it contains.
  • Discussion content and author attributions on forum indexes, forum threads, GeekLists, images, videos, files, and blog posts so blocked names, posts, placeholders, and quotations can be removed.
  • Text BGG inserts into the reply editor after you click Quote so quotations from blocked users, including their preceding nested quotations, can be removed before you post.
  • Your logged-in BGG authorization value, used only in page memory to call BGG’s own application programming interface (API). It is never saved or exposed to the extension’s isolated scripts.
  • Local extension state, including blocked usernames, settings, synchronization times, and removal counts.

Nothing is sent to Terwox or any third party. Reply drafts are processed only in the open page and are never stored by the extension. There are no ads, analytics, telemetry, or remote code.

Read the privacy policy

If you do not agree, close this tab. The extension will remain inactive and will not process BGG data.
